# StatBomb Deductions Images

12 standalone cinematic football-event images for the StatBomb-CMS `deductions` folder.

## Files

- `wrong-yellow-card.webp` — Incorrect yellow card recorded.
- `missing-yellow-card.webp` — Yellow card not recorded.
- `wrong-red-card.webp` — Incorrect red card recorded.
- `missing-red-card.webp` — Red card not recorded.
- `extra-assist.webp` — An extra assist was recorded.
- `missing-assist.webp` — An assist was not recorded.
- `wrong-substitution.webp` — Incorrect substitution recorded.
- `extra-substitution.webp` — An extra substitution was recorded.
- `missing-substitution.webp` — A substitution was not recorded.
- `wrong-tactical-shift.webp` — Incorrect tactical shift.
- `missing-tactical-shift.webp` — Tactical shift not recorded.
- `wrong-formation.webp` — Incorrect formation recorded.

Images were cropped from the supplied 12-panel generated contact sheet and saved as
individual WebP files at high quality. Their resolution is limited by the panel size
of the original contact sheet.
